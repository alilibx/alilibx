# Mastering Prompt Engineering for Professional Productivity

> **IEEE Sudan Workshop** · Online · 2 hours
> **Facilitator:** Ali Alhashimi — AI Solutions Architect, Generative AI Specialist

A hands-on, 2-hour workshop that takes participants from "typing questions into a
chatbot" to **engineering reliable prompts** for real academic and technical work.

---

## 📋 Agenda

1. **The Evolution of Generative AI**
2. **The Anatomy of an Effective Prompt**
3. **Advanced Prompting Patterns & Logic**
4. **Ethics, Accuracy, and Practical Application**

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this workshop, participants will be able to:

1. **The Perfect Prompt** — Structure any prompt using the core blocks:
   **Context · Task · Constraints · Format (CTCF)**.
2. **Advanced Tactics** — Apply professional frameworks: **Role–Task–Format (RTF)**
   and **Chain-of-Thought (CoT)** reasoning.
3. **High Accuracy** — Reduce hallucinations and biased outputs using grounding,
   verification, and self-critique techniques.
4. **Task Automation** — Speed up repetitive academic and technical work
   (summaries, code, emails, data extraction, study aids).

## 🧰 What participants need

- A free account on any chat assistant (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or Copilot).
- The **cheat sheet** open in a second window.
- One real task from their own study/work to practice on.

## 🧑‍🏫 What the facilitator needs

- Screen share + a model open for **live demos**.
- The **exercise prompts** pre-loaded so you can paste fast.
- A shared doc / chat channel where participants drop their attempts.

---

## 💡 Facilitation principles

- **Show, don't just tell.** Every concept gets a live before/after demo.
- **Bad prompt → good prompt.** The contrast is the lesson.
- **Make them type.** People learn prompting by doing it, not watching it.
- **Model-agnostic.** Techniques work across Claude, GPT, Gemini, etc.
